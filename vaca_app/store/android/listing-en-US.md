# Google Play Listing — VACA

## App name

VACA

## Short description

Save incident signals offline and deliver them when connectivity returns.

## Full description

VACA is an offline-first civic application for preserving incident signals when connectivity is unavailable or unreliable.

It lets people save a structured signal locally, maintain a durable delivery queue, synchronize when connectivity returns, and verify technical server receipt through a signed receipt. Location, photos, and identity verification remain optional for urgent local capture.

VACA clearly distinguishes local storage, technical server receipt, and institutional response. It does not guarantee rescue, dispatch, benefits, or assistance. Always use official emergency channels when they are available.

Advertising is limited to the informational “About VACA” screen. Ads never interrupt incident creation, updates, cancellation, delivery, identity verification, or receipt review. Incident content, precise location, evidence, and identity data are not shared with advertising networks.

## RevenueCat Peace Prize statement

VACA was designed to reduce information loss during disasters and connectivity failures. It helps a person preserve a structured signal on their device, deliver it when a network path becomes available, and verify technical receipt without making identity, connectivity, or ability to pay a barrier to requesting help. Its social value is continuity, transparency, and safer coordination under degraded conditions.
